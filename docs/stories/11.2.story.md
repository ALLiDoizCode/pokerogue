# Story 11.2: Move-Based Transformation Migration

**Epic:** Epic 11 - Dynamic Form Change System  
**Status:** Done  
**Created:** September 27, 2025  
**Assigned:** Move Transformation Engineering Team  

## Story Statement
As a **move transformation engineer**,  
I want **move-triggered form changes migrated to stateless AO process**,  
so that **move-based transformations maintain identical trigger timing and effect behavior**.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Move-triggered transformation timing maintains identical activation relative to move execution
2. Transformation stat modifications maintain mathematical precision during move calculation
3. Move transformation duration tracking maintains identical turn counting and expiration logic
4. Transform move interaction with abilities maintains identical priority and effect resolution
5. Multi-turn transformation effects maintain identical persistence and state management
6. Transform move accuracy and success rate calculations maintain identical probability behavior
7. Complex transformation scenarios maintain identical interaction resolution and outcomes
8. Comprehensive testing validates 100% move-based transformation behavioral parity

### Specific Move-Based Transformation Examples
- **Aegislash Stance Change**: Pre-move form changes based on move category [Source: src/data/pokemon-forms.ts:398-399]
  - Shield form: High defenses (150/150), activated by King's Shield or defensive moves
  - Blade form: High attacks (150/150), triggered by offensive moves (non-STATUS category)
  - Requires Stance Change ability, reverts when switching out
- **Meloetta Relic Song**: Post-move toggle between Aria and Pirouette forms [Source: src/data/pokemon-forms.ts:380]
  - Aria form: Normal/Psychic, higher Sp.Attack/Sp.Defense focus
  - Pirouette form: Normal/Fighting, higher Attack/Speed focus
  - Blocked if ability is Sheer Force or in Single Type challenge
- **Transform Move**: Complete species transformation copying target [Source: src/data/moves/move.ts:7760]
  - Copies target's species, type, stats, moveset, and abilities
  - Duration: Until switching out or battle end
  - Cannot copy certain moves (Struggle, Transform, etc.)
- **Keldeo Secret Sword**: Form change on move learning/forgetting [Source: src/data/pokemon-forms.ts:376-377]
  - Ordinary → Resolute when Secret Sword is learned
  - Resolute → Ordinary when Secret Sword is forgotten
  - Form persistence across battles until move status changes
