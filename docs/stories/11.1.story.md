# Story 11.1: Conditional Form Change Migration

**Epic:** Epic 11 - Dynamic Form Change System  
**Status:** Done  
**Created:** September 27, 2025  
**Assigned:** Form Change Engineering Team  

## Story Statement
As a **form change engineer**,  
I want **conditional Pokemon form change systems migrated to stateless AO process**,  
so that **form transformation triggers and conditions maintain identical evaluation behavior**.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. All form change trigger conditions evaluate identically to TypeScript implementation
2. Form change stat recalculation maintains mathematical precision for all transformations
3. Form change ability updates maintain identical assignment and activation behavior
4. Form change move pool updates maintain identical move list modifications and restrictions
5. Form change visual representation maintains identical sprite and animation updates
6. Form change persistence maintains identical state tracking across battle and storage
7. Complex form change chains maintain identical sequence logic and state transitions
8. Comprehensive testing validates 100% conditional form change behavioral parity

### Specific Form Change Examples
- **Darmanitan Zen Mode**: HP ≤ 50% triggers Standard → Zen form change [Source: src/data/abilities/ability.ts:7284]
  - Standard Mode: Fire type, 140 Attack, 95 Speed
  - Zen Mode: Fire/Psychic, 30 Attack, 140 Sp.Attack, 55 Speed
  - Galarian variant: Ice → Ice/Fire in Zen Mode
- **Castform Weather Forms**: Forecast ability + weather conditions [Source: src/data/pokemon-forms/form-change-triggers.ts:237]
  - Normal form: No weather or suppressed
  - Sunny form: Harsh sunlight (Fire type)
  - Rainy form: Rain (Water type)
  - Snowy form: Snow/Hail (Ice type)
- **Meloetta Relic Song**: Using Relic Song toggles Aria ↔ Pirouette [Source: src/data/pokemon-forms.ts:380]
  - Aria form: Normal/Psychic, higher Sp.Attack
  - Pirouette form: Normal/Fighting, higher Attack/Speed
  - Blocked if ability is Sheer Force or in Single Type challenge
- **Aegislash Stance Change**: Move-based Shield ↔ Blade forms
  - Shield form: High defenses (150/150)
  - Blade form: High attacks (150/150), triggered by offensive moves
- **Cramorant Gulp Missile**: Ability-triggered based on HP [Source: src/data/pokemon-forms.ts:501]
  - Gulping form: HP ≥ 50% after using Surf/Dive
  - Gorging form: HP < 50% after using Surf/Dive
